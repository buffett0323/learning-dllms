Nemotron-Labs-Sigma ([notes](../../papers/sigma/)) and NLD are dLLMs trained with an AR loss alongside (M12), so one backbone has both a diffusion head and a causal AR head. One round: the diffusion head drafts a block of 32 from pure noise in one call, and the AR head verifies it in one causal pass. One model, one KV cache.

The output is the AR head's greedy text, so the quality is that of the model's **AR mode**, not its diffusion mode: for Sigma-8B, 67.48 instead of 71.16 on its base benchmarks.

Two cautions on the reported numbers. Speedups are against the **same model's** AR decoding, and a round costs 3.0–3.5 AR steps, not 2, because both passes cover the whole block. And in absolute tokens per second, Sigma and NLD are within a few percent of each other, while NLD's AR head is more accurate (GSM8K 88.8 against 86.4 at 8B). Sigma's drafts are accepted more, but that buys no clear win.
