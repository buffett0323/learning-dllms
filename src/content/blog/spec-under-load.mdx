At batch 1 a forward pass is memory-bound, so checking 16 extra positions costs almost nothing (P08). With many requests in flight it becomes compute-bound, and every rejected position takes compute another request could have used:

- DFlash on SGLang, Qwen3-8B, MATH-500: 5.1× with 1 request, 2.8× with 32.
- DFlash 2 on SGLang, Qwen3.8-27B, GSM8K: 3.43× with 1 request, 1.45× with 32. Qwen's MTP heads: 1.04× with 32.
- EAGLE-3 with a 60-node draft tree on LLaMA-3.1-8B: 1.9× with 1 request, **0.6×** with 32, slower than no speculation.

So how much to verify per request is a scheduling decision that depends on load. DFlash notes that a drafter trained with blocks of 16 also works with 8, so the block could shrink when the server is busy, and leaves it there.
